# Code of Conduct

Kachel follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## In short

- Be welcoming and respectful, whatever someone's experience, background or
  identity.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing others'
  private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you act on
behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who
don't follow this. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer
through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/jklaiber).
